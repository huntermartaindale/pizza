How the yeast amount is calculated

Yeast is the hard part: there is no single published formula, so this tool uses the dough.school yeast table and a Q10 fermentation model (activity rises ~2.5× per 10 °C). A two-stage room + fridge rise is collapsed into one "effective" room-temperature time, then the required instant-dry-yeast percentage is read off the table and converted to your yeast type. Treat it as a well-grounded estimate, not a guarantee - dough also depends on flour, salt, and your kitchen.

Open source dough math. Measurements default to grams; a kitchen scale is most accurate.
